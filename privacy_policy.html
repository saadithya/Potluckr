1. Introduction

Potluckr ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Potluckr mobile application (the "App"), a group meal-planning app for friends, family, roommates and flatmates. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use and share it, how we protect it, and the choices and rights you have. It applies to all users of the App and is written to comply with applicable privacy laws — including the GDPR and CCPA/CPRA — and Google Play's User Data policies.

By creating an account or using the App, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy. Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it at any time as described below.

2. Information We Collect

Account information you provide

  • Your name and email address when you register with email and password.
  • Basic profile information (your name and email address) when you choose "Continue with Google". We receive this from Google only after you authorize the sign-in; we never receive or see your Google password.
  • A securely hashed password when you register with email. Passwords are managed by Firebase Authentication and are never stored or visible in plain text.

Content you create in the App

  • Recipes and recipe photos, groups and group memberships, meal plans, cooking events, votes, meal reviews and ratings, shopping lists, and shared pantry items.

Information collected automatically

  • Limited technical and usage data (such as device type, app version, and crash reports) that is processed by Firebase to keep the App reliable and secure.

We do not collect precise location, contacts, payment card numbers, health data, or browsing history, and we do not use advertising identifiers. The App may ask for permission to access your camera or photo library solely so you can attach photos to recipes; you can decline without losing other features.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account and authenticate you (including via Google Sign-In).
  • To provide core features: group cooking, recipe sharing, meal planning, voting, shopping lists, and pantry tracking.
  • To show the content you share to the members of the groups you join or create.
  • To send you in-app notifications about group activity, invitations, and cooking turns.
  • To maintain security, prevent abuse, diagnose crashes, and improve the App.

Where the GDPR applies, our legal bases are: performance of a contract (providing the App you request), your consent (optional features such as Google Sign-In and photo access), and our legitimate interests (security, reliability, and service improvement).

4. How We Share Information

  • Within your groups: content you share in a group (recipes, meal plans, shopping lists, votes, reviews, and your display name) is visible to the other members of that group.
  • With service providers: we use Google Firebase for authentication and identity, and we operate our own secure application servers and database to store your content. These providers process data under their own privacy terms and contractual safeguards.
  • For legal reasons: if required by law, regulation, or valid legal process.

We do not sell your personal information, we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we do not use third-party advertising networks.

6. Data Storage & Security

Your authentication identity is managed by Firebase Authentication. Your App content is stored on servers we operate and control. Data is encrypted in transit using TLS, and access is protected by authentication and server-side authorization rules. While we use industry-standard safeguards, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

7. Retention & Deletion

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active. You can permanently delete your account and associated personal data at any time — directly from the App (Profile → Delete Account) or from the web, without contacting us:

Delete your account and data: https://potluckr.airozgar.com/delete-account

Deletion is self-service and immediate. It removes your profile, recipes, pantry items, meal plans you created, and invitations you sent, and scrubs your personal references from shared records. Content you shared with a group may remain visible to that group in anonymized form. If you need help deleting data or exercising a right, email us (see Section 13); verified requests are completed within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

8. Your Rights & Choices

  • Access, correct, or update your account information from within the App or by contacting us.
  • Request deletion of your account and personal data (see Section 7).
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing, and withdraw consent at any time.
  • California residents: you have the right to know, delete, and correct personal information, and to opt out of its "sale" or "sharing" — we do neither.
  • EEA/UK residents: you may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any right, email us (see Section 13). We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law (generally 30 days).

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Third-Party Services

The App relies on Google Firebase (Authentication and related infrastructure) and optional Google Sign-In. Their handling of data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy) and Firebase's privacy and security documentation (firebase.google.com/support/privacy). We encourage you to review them.

11. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed on servers located outside your country, including in the United States, where our service providers operate. Where required, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, for material changes, notify you in the App or by email. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

13. Contact Us

For privacy questions, requests, or concerns, contact us at privacy@airozgar.com. For general support, email support@airozgar.com.
